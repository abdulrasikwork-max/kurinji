Phase 13F · design direction for review

Kurinji

குறிஞ்சி

Written for the next one who goes.

A field journal from the hills, not a feed. Every screen is built from three facts about a post: where, when and what the next person should know. Each of those gets its own object: a postmark, a band of time and a ticket.

The colours come from the Western Ghats. Neelakurinji blooms violet-blue across the hills once every twelve years. Tamil Nadu's state butterfly, the Tamil Yeoman, is a tawny orange with dark wing edges. Indian office stamps are inked in violet.

Nothing here is built yet. The screens are mock-ups for this review. Sample place, names and text are examples, not data.

The system

Colour roles
Mist · the page. Cool hill mist, not cream paper.
Ink · indigo night. All body text.
Bloom · Kurinji violet-blue. Brand, buttons, postmarks, "Been".
Yeoman · intent only: the butterfly and "Want to go". A fill, never small text.
Ochre · moderation notices, always with an icon and words.
Red · emergency screens only. Unchanged rule.

Dark mode is "Kurinji night" (the kurinji landscape's hour in Sangam poetry is midnight). Every text pair is 4.5:1 or better in both themes.

Alternative display: Arima, which also has Tamil letters. Fonts ship inside the app (OFL licence); nothing loads from the network.

Four motifs, each with a job
Postmark = where and when. Place name on the ring, month and time of day below. Area posts show a grid reference, city posts only the city, "no place" posts a blank cancellation.
Ticket = the tip for the next person. Notched like a bus ticket, because it is what gets someone there.
Butterfly and chrysalis = intent. Want to go is a chrysalis in your Cocoon; Been is the butterfly.
Kolam = empty states only. A kolam is drawn at the door every morning for whoever comes next.

Motion: four moments, nothing else moves

Butterfly to CocoonTwo wing beats, then a curved flight into the Cocoon tab, which pulses once. Light haptic.~600 ms · reduce motion: state change + spoken "Saved to your Cocoon"
EmergenceOn "I've been" the chrysalis splits and the butterfly opens its wings, then the invitation sheet rises.~700 ms · reduce motion: cross-fade
Stamp on shareThe postmark presses onto the page with a small overshoot and settles at a slant.~280 ms · haptic · reduce motion: appears
KolamEmpty states draw their kolam line once, then rest. No loops, no ambient movement.~900 ms once · reduce motion: drawn

Screen changes use a short fade with a 12 dp rise (about 220 ms). Cards respond to touch with a slight press. All of it follows the system "remove animations" setting.

What I need you to decide

Brand on screen. Show "Kurinji" as the app name and in every visible text now, keeping the package name com.safesphere.app. Trademark, domain and store checks are still open.
Fonts. Download Young Serif and Mukta Malar (OFL) from the official Google Fonts repository into the app, about 0.7 MB. Without them the app falls back to the phone's own serif and sans.
SOS shortcut and Quick Settings tile. Open the SOS screen with the 5-second countdown already running, or open it waiting for a tap.
"Let someone know". A message the person edits and sends from their own app, with no location and no link. Kurinji sends and stores nothing.
